Design and Implementation of a Past Questions and Solution Repository System for Science-
Based Courses
Project Description
This project involves the development of an online repository system for compiling past examination
questions and detailed solutions for science-related courses over the past twenty-five years. The system

is designed to help students access previous examination questions, study question patterns, understand
repeated concepts, and prepare better for examinations through well-explained solutions.
In many universities, past questions are usually scattered among students, photocopy vendors,
departments, class representatives, and informal WhatsApp groups. This makes it difficult for students
to find complete, accurate, and organized past questions for their courses. Some students may only
have access to a few years of questions, while others may not get reliable solutions or proper
explanations. This project aims to solve that problem by creating a centralized digital platform where
past questions and their solutions can be stored, searched, and accessed easily.
The system will cover all courses in science-related departments. Students will be able to select their
department, level, semester, and course code. After selecting a course, they can view past questions
arranged by year or academic session. They can also check the solution to each question, including
step-by-step explanations where necessary. This will help students not only know the answers but also
understand the concepts behind the answers.
A major feature of the system is the question trend analysis. The system can identify commonly
repeated topics, frequently asked question types, and areas that appear often in examinations. For
example, if a particular topic has appeared several times in the last ten years, the system can show
students that the topic is important and likely worth serious revision. This will support smarter and
more focused studying.
The system will also allow administrators or authorized lecturers to upload past questions and
solutions. Each question can be tagged by course, topic, year, semester, department, difficulty level,
and question type. This will make it easier for students to search for questions based on topic or course.
The project is not intended to promote examination malpractice. Instead, it is designed as an academic
revision and learning support system. Its purpose is to help students study better, understand course
trends, improve their preparation, and develop stronger problem-solving skills.
Aim of the Project
The aim of this project is to develop an online past questions and solution repository system that allows
students in science-related departments to access, study, and analyze examination questions and
solutions from the past twenty-five years.
The project will
1. be a centralized digital platform for storing past questions and solutions.
2. Allow students to search for past questions by department, course code, level, semester, year,
and topic.
3. Provide detailed solutions and explanations for each question.
4. Enable students to view examination question trends over the past twenty-five years.
5. Allow administrators or authorized users to upload, edit, and manage past questions.
6. Tag questions according to topic, year, course, difficulty level, and question type.
7. Help students identify frequently repeated topics and important areas of study.
8. Improve students’ access to academic revision materials.
9. Reduce dependence on scattered, incomplete, or unreliable past question materials.
10. Provide a user-friendly system for academic learning and examination preparation.

The lecturer or content manager will upload past questions, provide verified solutions, tag questions by
topic, and approve materials before students can access them. The student will search for courses, view
past questions, study solutions, check topic trends, and use the system for examination preparation. The
source of the solution should be stated. It might be from the lecturer, student or from online.
Core Features of the System
 Student registration and login
 Admin login
 Department and course management
 Past question upload
 Solution upload
 Course search by course code or title
 Filter by year, semester, level, and topic
 Detailed answer explanations
 Question trend analysis
 Frequently repeated topic display
 Topic-based question grouping
 Download or online viewing option
 Bookmark or save question feature
 Admin approval before publication
 Search history and usage records
 Dashboard showing available courses and materials
Example System Flow
A student logs into the platform and selects the Faculty of Science. The student then chooses their
department, level, semester, and course code. The system displays all available past questions for that
course from the past twenty-five years.
The student can choose to view questions by year, such as 2001, 2005, 2010, 2018, or 2024. The
student can also filter by topic, such as organic chemistry, calculus, genetics, mechanics, statistics,
microbiology, or thermodynamics, depending on the course.
When the student opens a question, the system displays the question, the correct solution, and a detailed
explanation. If the student wants to understand examination patterns, the system can show the most
frequently asked topics and the number of times each topic has appeared over the years.
For example, if “Differentiation” appears in mathematics exams fifteen times in twenty-five years, the
system can mark it as a high-frequency topic. This helps the student know which areas require more
attention during revision.
Expected Outcome
At the end of the project, the group should have a working online system where past questions and
solutions for science-related courses can be uploaded, organized, searched, and accessed by students.
The system should help students prepare better for examinations by providing past questions, detailed
solutions, topic-based filtering, and examination trend analysis.
